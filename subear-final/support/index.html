Subear Support

For app issues, billing questions, feedback, or feature requests, use the support channel below.

  • Issue tracker: github.com/ZhangDo/subear-final/issues

Before contacting support

  • Check that microphone and speech recognition permissions are enabled.
  • Open Language Packs and refresh the source and target language pair.
  • For subscriptions, use Restore Purchases in Settings.
