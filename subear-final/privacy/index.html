Subear Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

Audio

Subear uses the microphone only while a live interpretation session is running. Audio recordings are not stored by the app and are not synced to iCloud.

Transcript History

Transcript text, translations, session titles, notes, favorites, and language preferences are stored on device. If iCloud is available, text history and preferences may sync through the user's private iCloud database.

Purchases

Subscription purchases and billing are handled by the App Store. Subear does not receive or store payment card details.

Tracking

Subear does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies and does not use tracking for advertising.

Support

For privacy questions or support requests, use the support contact listed on the Subear support page.
