Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Your TV stays on your network.
你的电视画面只留在你的网络里。

English

The short version

TVScope does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer runs no server that the app talks to.

Local network

TVScope uses local network access to find Apple TV devices on the network you are already on, to pair with them, and to receive their screen and sound. This traffic goes directly between your device and your Apple TV. Nothing leaves that network.

Screenshots and recordings

Screenshots and recordings are written to the app's own storage on your device. They go to Photos only when you turn on Save to Photos or choose Save to Photos yourself. TVScope asks only for permission to add to Photos, not to read your library.

Microphone and camera

The microphone is used only while a recording is running, and only when Record Microphone is on. Your voice is mixed into that recording on the device. TVScope does not record video from your device's camera.

Pairing information

Pairing keys for your Apple TV are kept in the device Keychain. Device names, addresses and settings are kept in the app's storage. None of this is sent to the developer.

Diagnostics

Diagnostic logs contain device names, timings and error codes. They never contain screen contents. They stay on your device until you choose to share them, for example by attaching them to a support message.

Purchases

If TVScope offers in-app purchases, payment is handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment details.

Children

TVScope does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions, use the contact details on the support page.

简体中文

简要说明

TVScope 不会收集、传输或出售任何个人信息。没有账号，没有统计分析，没有广告，也没有跟踪。App 不连接开发者运营的任何服务器。

本地网络

TVScope 使用本地网络权限，在你当前所在的网络中查找 Apple TV，与其配对，并接收它的画面和声音。这些数据只在你的设备和 Apple TV 之间直接传输，不会离开该网络。

截图和录制

截图和录制保存在本设备上 App 自己的储存空间中。只有在你开启“存储到照片”或手动选择“存储到照片”时，才会写入照片图库。TVScope 只申请向照片添加内容的权限，不读取你的图库。

麦克风和相机

麦克风只在录制进行中、并且开启“录制麦克风”时使用，你的声音会在本设备上混入这段录制。TVScope 不会用设备的相机录制视频。

配对信息

Apple TV 的配对密钥保存在设备的钥匙串中。设备名称、地址和设置保存在 App 的储存空间中。这些信息都不会发送给开发者。

诊断信息

诊断日志包含设备名称、耗时和错误代码，从不包含屏幕内容。日志会一直保存在你的设备上，直到你主动共享，例如在联系支持时附上。

购买

如果 TVScope 提供 App 内购买，付款由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。开发者不会收到或保存你的支付信息。

儿童

TVScope 不会有意收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会在此页面发布新版本并更新生效日期。如有隐私问题，请通过支持页面中的联系方式与我们联系。
